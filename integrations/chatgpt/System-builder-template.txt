<section class="method-grid"><article class="panel"><span class="eyebrow">SOLVER SEMANTICS</span><h2>What happens at each sample?</h2><ol><li>State-only blocks expose their current state: Integrator, Unit delay, and the two plant types.</li><li>Sources and direct-feedthrough blocks evaluate in dependency order. The PID uses the current setpoint and measurement, its stored integral, and a filtered measurement derivative.</li><li>Scopes record that instant’s outputs. Inputs are held while continuous plants integrate with RK4 substeps.</li><li>Every state updates synchronously. The sample index advances by exactly one dt.</li></ol><p>Feedback through a plant or another state is supported. A loop made only from Gain, Sum, Saturation, PID, or other direct-feedthrough blocks is rejected as an <strong>algebraic loop</strong>. Add a delay only if that delay belongs in the system you intend to model.</p></article><article class="panel"><span class="eyebrow">EVIDENCE & LIMITS</span><h2>Use the result at its actual scope.</h2><p>This editor implements scalar signal flow. It does not infer signal units, solve acausal mechanical or electrical connections, import Xcos diagrams, or generate deployed controller code.</p><p>The mass plant uses N, kg, m, N·s/m, and N/m. Other signal units depend on your model; PID gain units follow the input/output quantities. Document them in block and scope labels.</p><p>RK4 substeps resolve the declared plant rates, but the connected feedback still samples and holds signals at dt. Halve dt and compare responses; include real delays, noise, nonlinearities, and actuator dynamics before making engineering decisions.</p><p>Clocks pause when the browser tab is hidden. Delayed frames contribute at most 0.1 s wall time; solver steps remain fixed. Playback can slow under load; numerical steps are never skipped. Runs round up to the next sample and stop if a scalar/state exceeds the numerical envelope of 10⁸.</p><p><strong>Model results are illustrative. No physical hardware or safety function is validated.</strong></p></article></section>
