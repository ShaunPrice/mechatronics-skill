SOLVER SEMANTICS

What happens at each sample?

  1. State-only blocks expose their current state: Integrator, Unit delay, and the two plant types.
  2. Sources and direct-feedthrough blocks evaluate in dependency order. The PID uses the current setpoint and measurement, its stored integral, and a filtered measurement derivative.
  3. Scopes record that instant’s outputs. Inputs are held while continuous plants integrate with RK4 substeps.
  4. Every state updates synchronously. The sample index advances by exactly one dt.

Feedback through a plant or another state is supported. A loop made only from Gain, Sum, Saturation, PID, or other direct-feedthrough blocks is rejected as an algebraic loop. Add a delay only if that delay belongs in the system you intend to model.

EVIDENCE & LIMITS

Use the result at its actual scope.

This editor implements scalar signal flow. It does not infer signal units, solve acausal mechanical or electrical connections, import Xcos diagrams, or generate deployed controller code.

The mass plant uses N, kg, m, N·s/m, and N/m. Other signal units depend on your model; PID gain units follow the input/output quantities. Document them in block and scope labels.

RK4 substeps resolve the declared plant rates, but the connected feedback still samples and holds signals at dt. Halve dt and compare responses; include real delays, noise, nonlinearities, and actuator dynamics before making engineering decisions.

Clocks pause when the browser tab is hidden. Delayed frames contribute at most 0.1 s wall time; solver steps remain fixed. Playback can slow under load; numerical steps are never skipped. Runs round up to the next sample and stop if a scalar/state exceeds the numerical envelope of 10⁸.

Model results are illustrative. No physical hardware or safety function is validated.
